# Privacy Policy — Precise Player

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

Precise Player is a video player. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

- **No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
- **Your videos stay on your device.** The app reads the videos and subtitle files you choose to open, and (if you allow it) lists the videos on your device in the "Videos" tab. This happens only on your device.
- **Playback history** (recently opened files and where you stopped) and your settings are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. You can clear the history in the app at any time. If you use Android's backup, this may be included in your own device backup.
- **Saved frames** ("Save frame as image") are written to your Pictures folder on your device.
- **Network access** is used only to play a web address (URL) that you enter or open with the app. That connection goes directly to the server you chose; the app sends nothing else.
- **Permissions:** *Photos and videos / storage* — to list and play your videos. *Internet* — to play web addresses you open.

Children: the app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
